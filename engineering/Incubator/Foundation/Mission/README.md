## Purpose

The Mission defines what the Engineering Knowledge Framework exists to accomplish.

It expresses the framework's enduring commitment while remaining consistent with the Constitution and the Foundation.

---

## Mission Statement

> **The mission of the Engineering Knowledge Framework is to help engineers discover, organize, preserve, and communicate engineering knowledge so that understanding endures beyond the systems it creates.**

---

## Commitment

The Engineering Knowledge Framework is committed to treating engineering knowledge as an enduring engineering asset.

Through its Foundation, Framework, Standards, Templates, and supporting practices, EKF exists to help engineers preserve not only what they build, but also the understanding that gives their work meaning.

---

## Boundaries

The Mission defines what EKF is committed to accomplishing.

It does not establish constitutional principles, prescribe implementation details, define engineering standards, or describe the framework's long-term vision.
