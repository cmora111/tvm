# The Constitution

## The Foundational Principles of the Engineering Knowledge Framework

## Purpose

The Constitution defines the enduring identity and philosophy of EKF.

## Organization

- [Preamble](Preamble/)
- [Articles](Articles/)

## Authority

The Constitution is the highest foundational artifact within EKF. Framework models, principles, standards, templates, and publishing practices must remain consistent with it.

## Change Discipline

The Constitution changes only through deliberate review, a cooling period, and a recorded rationale.
