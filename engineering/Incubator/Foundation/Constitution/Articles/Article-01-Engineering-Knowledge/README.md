# Article I — Engineering Knowledge

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Status | Draft |
| Collection | Constitution |
| Primary Question | What is engineering knowledge? |

## Purpose

Article I will define engineering knowledge and establish it as an engineering asset.

## Working Declaration

> **Engineering knowledge is the accumulated understanding that enables engineers to make informed decisions throughout the lifecycle of an engineered system.**

## Boundaries

This Article will address the nature, decision-making value, and enduring worth of engineering knowledge.

It will not define stewardship responsibilities, standards, templates, publishing formats, or project-specific practices.

## Forge Status

The working declaration remains on the anvil for deliberate review.
