# Founder's Notebook

## Purpose

The Founder's Notebook preserves observations, instincts, questions, and emerging ideas discovered while forging the Engineering Knowledge Framework.

A Founder's Note is not yet an approved principle, standard, or Forge Ledger Strike. It provides a place for an idea to be preserved while experience and honest inspection determine whether it deserves a permanent role in EKF.

## Relationship to the Forge Ledger

The Founder's Notebook records ideas that are still being considered.

The Forge Ledger records enduring discoveries that have proven worthy of preservation.

```text
Observation
    ↓
Founder's Notebook
    ↓
Engineering Experience
    ↓
Honest Inspection
    ↓
Forge Ledger or another EKF artifact
```

Not every Founder's Note must graduate. Remaining a useful observation is an acceptable outcome.
